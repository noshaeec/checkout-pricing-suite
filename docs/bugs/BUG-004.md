# BUG-004: Express delivery charge is dropped when standard shipping is free

- **Requirement:** REQ-09 (also affects REQ-10 and REQ-11)
- **Severity:** Major
- **Found by test:** `tests/test_express.py::test_express_adds_50_even_when_shipping_is_free`

## Steps to reproduce
1. Check out one item: name "widget", price 2000.00, quantity 1, no coupon, express delivery (`express=True`).
2. Read `receipt.shipping`, `receipt.vat` and `receipt.total`.

## Expected result (from the spec)
- The subtotal is 2000.00. The volume discount is 15% (REQ-04), which is 300.00, so the discounted subtotal is 1700.00.
- 1700.00 is at least R750.00, so standard shipping is free (REQ-08).
- Express delivery adds R50.00 on top of the shipping amount, always, even when standard shipping is free (REQ-09). `receipt.shipping` should be 50.00.
- VAT is 15% of (1700.00 - 0.00 + 50.00), which is 262.50 (REQ-10).
- The total should be 1700.00 + 50.00 + 262.50 = 2012.50 (REQ-11).

## Actual result
- `receipt.shipping` is 0.00. The R50.00 express charge is missing.
- `receipt.vat` is 255.00, which is 15% of 1700.00 only.
- `receipt.total` is 1955.00.

The total is R57.50 too low: R50.00 of shipping plus R7.50 of VAT on it.

## Notes
Input used in the test: `checkout([Item("widget", Decimal("2000.00"), 1)], express=True)`.

The defect only appears when standard shipping is free. A R100.00 express order is charged correctly (R60.00 + R50.00 = R110.00, tested in `test_express_adds_50_to_standard_shipping`). The code appears to return zero for free shipping without checking the express option. The shop loses the express fee, and the VAT on it, on every free-shipping order that chooses express.

Not yet tested: by REQ-08 and REQ-09, any order with a discounted subtotal of R750.00 or more should show the same defect.
